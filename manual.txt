2 arg math:
    0b01MMMMAT imm/addr, addr 
    M=math op
        0=add 1=sub 2=mul 3=div
        4=mod 5=pow 6=log 7=root
        ...
        ...
    A=addr mode (0=immediate 1=mem[arg1]
    T=type (0=int 1=float)
1 arg math:
    0b10MMMM0T
    M=math op
        0=sin  1=cos  2=tan 3=asin
        4=acos 5=atan 6=...
    T=type (0=int 1=float)
    
branch:
    0b110CCCAT
    C=compare type
        0=eq  1=neq 2=lt  3=gt
        4=lte 5=gte
    A=arg2 addr mode (imm/addr)
    T=int/float
